# Code of Conduct

We want this community to be welcoming and constructive. Treat people with respect, assume good faith, accept constructive feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, publication of private information without consent, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

This code applies to project spaces and to conduct representing the project. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community.

Report concerns privately to [wafffllesss@gmail.com](mailto:wafffllesss@gmail.com). Please describe what happened and include relevant links if possible. Reports will be handled discreetly to the extent practical; do not disclose sensitive personal information publicly.
