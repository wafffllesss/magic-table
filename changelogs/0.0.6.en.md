# Features

- Play solo matches, control seats at the table, and resume a saved game.
- Customize your playmat with colors or images and choose how other players’ playmats appear.
- Review imported deck cards and warnings before using the deck.
- Explore what is new in each version in the update history.

# Fixes and adjustments

- Improved card layout and interactions at the table.
- Adjusted mulligans, deck imports, and hand presentation.
- Improved multiplayer connections and chat notifications.
